Source00 is a small Swiss non-profit association founded in 2025. We keep
privacy-minded, open-source building blocks running smoothly for classrooms,
community groups, and partner organisations.

## Focus areas

- Practical open-source maintenance so useful tools stay available
- Clear documentation and approachable support for volunteers
- Privacy-minded security basics that respect local data protection rules
- Steady, Swiss-based stewardship for the projects we look after

## Platforms under our wing

- **Vocab Trainer** – quick vocab practice sessions with optional offline use
- **Allergy Scanner** – ingredient lookups for Coop and Migros groceries with
	configurable allergen alerts
- **Vocab Importer** – bookmarklet-powered importer that pulls Quizlet sets
	straight into Vocab Trainer

## How we work

We collaborate with educators, NGOs, and volunteers. Our role is to maintain
the software, keep the documentation accessible, and make sure our partners can
deploy the tools without vendor lock-in or unwanted tracking. If a project
needs mentoring, we help new contributors get comfortable and stay active.
