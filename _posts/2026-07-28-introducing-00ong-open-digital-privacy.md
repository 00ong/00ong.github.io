## Who is 00.ong?
00.ong (operated by Source00) is a Swiss non-profit association. We maintain open-source software, host privacy-minded community services, and publish direct, no-nonsense guides that help everyday people understand and secure their digital presence.

## What We Do

### 1. Open Digital Stewardship
We keep practical, open-source building blocks running smoothly—from system cleaning engines like **Shield Rust Cleaner Pro** to educational tools like **Vocab Trainer**.

### 2. Mainstream Privacy Empowering Guides
Privacy shouldn't require a computer science degree. We craft short, opinionated guides to help you:
* Disable OS background telemetry (Windows 10/11)
* Configure hardened, privacy-first browser windows (Firefox, Brave, LibreWolf)
* Block trackers and ad profiling across your devices

### 3. Community-First Principles
We operate under strict Swiss non-profit governance. No venture capital, no ad tracking, no affiliate paywalls—just clean, reliable open digital tools.
